Dress Code

In general, we're setting a Cocktail Attire dress code for our wedding. That being said, we want you to wear something that you LOVE and we don't want you to spend money on an outfit for our wedding (unless you're looking for an excuse to shop). So please feel free to follow any dress code before Casual on this guide. Distinctive, singular outfit choices to show off your “uniquely you” style are highly recommended.

Local Sites

Planning on making some tourist stops in Houston? Downtown Houston is about 45 minutes away from our venue and has some great sites. The Houston Zoo is home to the world's best animal, the rhino. The Houston Museum District has world-class museums. We recommend the Houston Museum of Natural Science because of the dinosaurs and pretty rocks.

Our venue is about 30 minutes away from Galveston, on the Gulf of Mexico. Walk around this super adorable coastal town, hit the beach, or head to the Galveston Island Historic Pleasure Pier for roller coaster rides and games.

Only 20 minutes from our venue is the Kemah Boardwalk and the Kemah Lighthouse District with shopping, drinking, and dining.

Space Center is nearby and offers a number of space related exhibits, including a bunch of real rockets on display. Take the tram tour and you will see some of the fam's work!

FAQ

Can I bring my children?

Yes, we would love for them to be part of our amazing weekend! Please include them in your RSVP.

What is the parking situation?

Ceremony: There is plenty of parking available at the venue, at 2nd St and Colorado Ave.

Farewell Brunch: Street parking anywhere in the neighborhood, but carpooling is highly recommended.

Are phones allowed?

During the ceremony, we ask that you put the phones away and let the photographer do her thing. After the event, we will share pictures with all of you!

Is alcohol allowed?

No outside alcohol is allowed at either the Rehearsal Dinner or the Ceremony/Reception. The Rehearsal dinner is at a brewery, where we will have some pre-purchased drink tickets. The Reception will include an open bar.

When should I arrive?

Doors for the ceremony venue will open at 5:00. The ceremony will start promptly at 5:30.

Something else?

Let us know, and we will find you an answer! We will update this FAQ periodically up until the event too.
